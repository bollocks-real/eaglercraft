About EaglyMC

EaglyMC is a Minecraft server running on version 1.20, offering a unique and enjoyable experience for all players. Join our community and start your adventure today!

How to Join

  1. Open Minecraft Java Edition (1.20+)
  2. Click on "Multiplayer"
  3. Add Server: play.eaglymc.fun
  4. Join and have fun!
